Do not use

Wording that must never appear in generated copy for a brand. Every entry is a blocker: any asset containing it fails QA and cannot be approved, and the phrase is stated to the model as a flat instruction before it sees any evidence.

Matching ignores case and extra spacing, and anchors on whole words — so ad will not flag “adjuster”. For a one-off that applies to a single campaign, use Do not mention on the brief instead of adding it here.
