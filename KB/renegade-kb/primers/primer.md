# Renegade Insurance - Brand Primer

Curated, not scraped. Written by hand from company-profile.md, locations.json and the corpus README. This is always supplied to the agent; everything else about Renegade is retrieved on demand.

## Identity

Renegade Insurance is a property & casualty insurance company running a retail agency network. It has two distinct customers:

- **Consumers**, who buy personal and commercial coverage through a local Renegade agent.
- **Agents**, who buy agency ownership through three programs: franchising, agency acquisition, and back-office book management.

Agents are independent rather than captive, so they place business across multiple carriers. Positioning is a marketplace (multiple carrier quotes) combined with a dedicated local agent.

## What it sells

**Personal lines:** home, auto, renters, motorcycle, wind, flood, pet, personal liability.

**Commercial lines:** general liability, professional liability, business owners policy, commercial auto, truck and transport, commercial property, cyber liability, workers' compensation, E&O, commercial umbrella, fiduciary.

**To agents:** franchise, agency sale and acquisition, back-office support.

**Carriers shown on the site (11):** AmTrust, ASI (Progressive), Chubb, Dairyland, The Hartford, Stillwater, US Assure, Travelers, Nationwide, Berkshire Hathaway Homestate, Universal Property.
