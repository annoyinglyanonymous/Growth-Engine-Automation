# We are Renegade

Renegade is a modern P&C insurance company built for agents who want ownership. We help agents start agencies, acquire agencies, and manage the books behind them.

OUR STORY

Renegade started with a simple problem. The insurance industry is full of talented agents, and too many of them grow, operate, and exit alone. Renegade built a different model, one grounded in ownership, community, and disciplined systems that hold up over the long term.

We believe the most trusted agencies come from people who know their communities and have real support behind them. That belief shapes every program Renegade runs and every owner Renegade backs.

Our Offerings

Designed for P&C agents at every stage of owning an agency: starting an agency, selling an agency, and back office support.

01/

### Franchise: Own a Renegade Agency

Renegade's franchise program gives licensed agents and career-changers a path to agency ownership starting at $20,000. Franchise owners earn 80% commission on new business, and Renegade runs the back office.

02/

### Mergers & Acquisitions: Sell Your Agency to Renegade

Renegade buys P&C agencies and books outright, with cash at close and no broker fees at any stage. Renegade's acquisition team builds a market-based valuation and structures deals with no earnout, no deferred pay, and no clawbacks.

03/

### Back Office Support: Keep Ownership, Hand Off the Work

Renegade manages an agent's book of business while the agent keeps full ownership. Renegade's operations team handles servicing, renewals, and day-to-day client support, and the agent keeps the client relationships and the equity in the book.

## FAQ

### What is Renegade Insurance?

Renegade Insurance is a modern P&C insurance company that helps agents own, sell, and manage insurance agencies. Renegade runs a franchise network, acquires agencies and books of business, and manages books for agents who keep ownership.

### What does Renegade Insurance do?

Renegade Insurance operates three core programs: agency franchising, agency acquisitions, and back office support. Renegade holds direct appointments with regional and national carriers and MGAs across personal lines, commercial lines, and specialty products.

### Where is Renegade Insurance located?

Renegade Insurance is headquartered in Beaverton, Oregon. Renegade operates 9 retail locations across Florida, South Carolina, Georgia, and Texas.

### How much does a Renegade franchise cost?

Renegade franchise fees start at $20,000 depending on business type. A monthly support and technology fee applies after launch, and financing is available for qualified candidates.

### How does Renegade value an agency it acquires?

Renegade builds a market-based valuation using retention ratio, commission revenue, growth trajectory, and book mix. Renegade acquires agencies with cash at close and no broker fees at any stage.

### Is Renegade Insurance a good place to work?

Renegade Insurance is a Great Place to Work Certified company. Renegade’s team works across its Oregon headquarters, its retail locations, and its operations and technology partners.
