## Frequently asked questions

### Is Orlando the only office Renegade has?

Our flagship office is in Atlanta, GA with multiple agency hubs throughout the southern and southeastern U.S. Our Orlando office is the primary hub for Florida and the southeastern seaboard.

### Is Renegade licensed?

Yes, Renegade is licensed in 48 states for Property and Casualty. We focus on Home, Auto, Commercial and Specialty. For more information, visit this link.

### How is Renegade different from other agencies?

Renegade combines the power of an insurance marketplace with an expert advisor. We help you get multiple quotes from leading carriers to choose from while providing you the guidance when and where you need it.

### How is a Renegade agent different from a captive agent?

Renegade agents operate as brokers. They have the capability to shop the entire marketplace to find the best product that fits your specific situation. Whether it is your personal belongings, or the most complicated commercial risk, we have the capability of making sure you are well taken care of.

### How will Renegade help me with Orlando-specific insurance problems?

Our team of local, Orlando-based advisors brings a wealth of knowledge and experience, ensuring every individual in the Orlando area receives top-notch care. Our company size grants us access to markets that smaller agencies may lack, providing you and your family with the best possible coverage and service.
