### What is Renegade Insurance?

Renegade Insurance is a modern P&amp;C insurance company that helps agents own, sell, and manage insurance agencies. Renegade runs a franchise network, acquires agencies and books of business, and manages books for agents who keep ownership.

`source: https://renegadeinsurance.com/about-us/`

### What does Renegade Insurance do?

Renegade Insurance operates three core programs: agency franchising, agency acquisitions, and back office support. Renegade holds direct appointments with regional and national carriers and MGAs across personal lines, commercial lines, and specialty products.

`source: https://renegadeinsurance.com/about-us/`

### What steps are involved in transferring my insurance agency/book of business to Renegade?

The process has four steps:
1. Renegade signs an NDA and issues a Letter of Intent.
2. Renegade requests a due diligence package including 12 months of commission statements, bank statements, production reports, loss ratio reports, a full policy list, carrier contact details, your active license, and current appointment status.
3. Once due diligence is complete, Renegade issues a contract for signing.
4. After signing, Renegade transfers payment and takes ownership of the book.

`source: https://renegadeinsurance.com/sell-your-insurance-agency/`
