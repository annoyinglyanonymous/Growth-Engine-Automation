## Frequently Asked Questions

### What steps are involved in transferring my insurance agency/book of business to Renegade?

The process has four steps:

1. Renegade signs an NDA and issues a Letter of Intent.

2. Renegade requests a due diligence package including 12 months of commission statements, bank statements, production reports, loss ratio reports, a full policy list, carrier contact details, your active license, and current appointment status.

3. Once due diligence is complete, Renegade issues a contract for signing.

4. After signing, Renegade transfers payment and takes ownership of the book.

### How long does it typically take to transfer an insurance agency/book of business with Renegade?

Most book transfers complete in 30 to 120 days from contract signing. The timeline depends on carrier requirements and administrative procedures. Renegade works to expedite the process while ensuring a smooth transition for all parties.

### What happens to my clients/customers after I transfer my insurance agency/book of business?

All clients transfer to Renegade, where the service team manages renewals, endorsements, cancellations, and cross-sells. Coverage continuity is maintained throughout the transition. Clients experience no gap in service.

### Do I have to use a broker to sell my insurance agency?

No. Renegade buys directly from agency owners with no broker involvement. There are no broker fees at any stage of the transaction.

### What if I'm not ready to sell my agency yet?

Renegade offers a Back Office Support partnership for agency owners who want to reduce operational load without a full exit. Partners retain their book while Renegade handles servicing, back-office, and carrier access.
