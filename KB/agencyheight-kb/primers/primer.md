# Agency Height - Brand Primer

Curated, not scraped. Written by hand from about-us.md, home.md and the corpus README. This is always supplied to the agent; everything else about Agency Height is retrieved on demand.

## Identity

Agency Height is a B2B software and lead-generation platform for independent insurance agents, founded in 2019.

**It does not sell insurance.** Its own about page opens with "We Don't Sell Insurance. We Build Connections." Any copy implying that Agency Height underwrites, quotes, or binds coverage is factually wrong about the business.

**Primary audience:** independent insurance agents and agencies. **Secondary:** consumers looking for an agent, who get routed to agents on the platform.

## Do not confuse with Renegade Insurance

Both brands share the corporate address 9450 SW Gemini Dr, PMB 47941, Beaverton, OR 97008. They are separate brands with different products, different audiences and different claims. Renegade sells insurance; Agency Height sells tools and leads to the people who sell insurance. Never ground Agency Height copy in Renegade material.

## Products

- **Markets** - carrier search across admitted and E&S options, filtered by line, appetite and state. Free on every plan.
- **Agent Directory** - free agent profile, surfaced by licence, state and coverage.
- **Intake Forms** - coverage-specific, bilingual lead capture, synced to the CRM.
- **Lead Bank** - unclaimed buyer leads, claimable exclusively on paid plans.
- **CRM** - pipeline from Suspect to Prospect to Customer. Free on every plan.
- **Website** - AI-built, SEO-optimised, live within a week.
- **InstaQuote**, **Lead Generator and Enricher**.

## Unverified figures - do not restate as fact

Agency Height's own marketing claims, reproduced from site copy and never independently verified. These also disagree with each other, so treat any specific agent count as unsupported.

- "2800+ licensed insurance agents" (car insurance page)
- "5k+" (car insurance page, referent unclear)

- "Thousands of top-rated independent agents" (about page)
