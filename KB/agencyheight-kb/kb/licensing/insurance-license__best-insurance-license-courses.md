## What to Look for in an Insurance Licensing Course

Before going through a possible list of recommendations, let’s set the standard for what makes a licensing course worthwhile:

- A robust curriculum: the course should cover all the topics as per your state’s exam outline, not leaving any gaps in knowledge.

- Engaging delivery means: video lectures, interactive exercises, practical case scenarios, etc. Learning the content should lead to converting it into long-term memories.

- Flexible Study options: Find programs that work with your schedule and your learning preferences, whether it’s a self-paced online learning environment or a structured virtual classroom.

- Practice exams and questions: The best course provides an ample number of practice questions and exams that simulate the same content and formats as the actual licensing exam.

- Pass guarantee: Many of the best providers now have a pass guarantee where a candidate can retake the exam if they didn’t pass the 1st time without any additional fees.

- Industry relevance: The content should be updated on a regular basis to ensure relevance to emerging regulations, products, and industry practices.

- Resource of support: Instructors, community forums, other studies, or community resources could prove valuable when preparing for the exam.

## How to Choose the Right Course for You

With so many great options, you may be thinking, “How do I decide which option is best for me?” Consider:

- Your learning style: Do you prefer to read, to watch videos, or prefer to do interactive exercises? Select a provider whose primary delivery format fits your preferred learning style.

- Timeframe: How soon do you need to be licensed? Some learning programs are created to be finished quickly, while others were created to be done more slowly.

- Your experience level: If you are a completely novice, you may want more extensive and comprehensive programs as it is your first time learning about the renewal process and other industry questions. If you have considerable experience and industry knowledge, then exam only solutions may work for you.

- Your budget: It is wise to invest in your education but be mindful that education opportunities vary at price points with varying levels of quality.

- Your future: If you are planning a career, select providers that offer continuation pathways for additional certifications and designations.
