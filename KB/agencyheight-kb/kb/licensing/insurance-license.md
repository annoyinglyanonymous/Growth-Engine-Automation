How much does an insurance license cost?

The cost of state licensing tests varies, but on average, they cost between $90-$350. The total cost of your insurance course, application, and exam in Texas and California will be around $375.

How long does it take for an insurance license to expire?

The expiration dates vary by state, although most have a two-year validity period. They can, however, be renewed within a limited time.
