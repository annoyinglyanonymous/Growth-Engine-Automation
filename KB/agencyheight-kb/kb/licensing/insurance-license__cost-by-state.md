### Examination Cost

It’s crucial to have your legal name, address, Social Security number, and daytime phone number ready before signing up for your exam. The name or school code of the institution where you finished your pre-licensing training is also necessary, if relevant. The examination fee is $63.

### Insurance License Renewal

Insurance licenses in Georgia expire every two years. The cost to renew your license is $100 per application.

### Examination Cost

The Department will give you instructions on how to schedule your exam after approving your application. Upon scheduling, a $44 examination fee is required. In Florida, life, accident, and health insurance is combined with property and casualty insurance. As a result, you would have to take the exam that suits the insurance lines you intend to carry.

### Insurance License Renewal

Florida grants permanent, lifetime insurance licenses. Each two-year renewal period, the State of Florida mandates that license holders who are residents complete twenty-four (24) credit hours of continuing education. After completing the necessary CE hours, your license can be renewed without paying a fee or submitting any documentation.

### Examination Cost

After finishing all of your pre-licensure education, the insurance exam is the next step. For each insurance line you want to carry, you will take a separate exam. You would only take two tests overall because the Life, Accident & Health, and Property & Casualty (P&C) lines are merged into one test apiece. The cost for each exam attempt is $55, while the cost for a public insurance adjuster is $68.

### Insurance License Renewal

Insurance licenses in California lapse every two years. The state doesn’t require you to take a new exam. However, you might take a quick knowledge test, depending on how your continuing education is structured. It will make sure that you comprehend key insurance principles, rules, and regulations. The cost to renew your California insurance license is $188 per line.

The insurance test is the next step after finishing all of your pre-license coursework or independent study. You will take a separate exam for each insurance line you want to carry.

FAQs

Is it mandatory to submit fingerprint impressions in California?

When applying for a non-resident license in California, you are not needed to provide fingerprints if you currently have a valid resident license issued by one of the following states:

Alaska, Idaho, Ohio, Texas, Arizona, Louisiana, Oregon, Utah, Delaware, Montana, Pennsylvania, Washington, New Jersey, South Carolina, Tennessee, Florida, Nevada, and Georgia.

What does obtaining an insurance license in Pennsylvania typically cost?

Pennsylvania charges $53 for the insurance license exam, $23.85 for fingerprinting, $55 for an online application, and $25 for any revisions.

Can I renew my insurance license for free after it expires?

To reinstate a license that has expired, a relicensing application must be submitted. The majority of states charge a nominal fee for late and regular renewal of licenses.

What are the non-resident insurance license fees by state?

According to the NIPR, there is a $5.60 transaction fee in addition to the state-wise fee to get a non-resident insurance license.
