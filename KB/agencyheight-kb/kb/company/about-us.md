# We Don’t Sell Insurance. We

Build Connections.

Insurance has always been personal — but somewhere along the way, it got buried under numbers, endless hold music, and faceless call centers. Buyers got frustrated. Agents got left behind.

That’s the gap Agency Height was built to close.

## Our Beginning

Back in 2019, we asked a simple question:

“Why is it easier to order a pizza than to find the right insurance agent?”

No one had a good answer. So we created one.

We launched Agency Height to flip the script — putting trust back into the insurance experience by making it easy for real people to find real agents.

## Our Story

#### 2019

##### Launched

Agency Height is founded with a mission to simplify insurance connections and give independent agents digital visibility.

#### 2021

##### Nationwide Coverage

Expanded directory listings to all 50 states, enabling buyers everywhere to find trusted local agents.

#### 2023

##### Rapid Growth

Surpassed 2,000 verified agents on the platform and introduced new lead-generation features to connect agents with more buyers.

#### 2024

##### Product Expansion

Released upgraded agent profiles with reviews and specialties, and added analytics dashboards for agents.

#### 2025

##### Premium Membership

Introduced Premium with unlimited leads, full contact details, higher search rankings, and business growth tools for ambitious agents.
