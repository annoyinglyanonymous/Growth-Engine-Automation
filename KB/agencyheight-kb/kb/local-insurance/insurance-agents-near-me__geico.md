Does GEICO use local agents?

GEICO operates differently from traditional insurance companies in terms of its distribution model. GEICO does not use independent insurance brokerages or agencies. Instead, it employs exclusive GEICO Local Agents who represent GEICO and its partner companies. These agents are not allowed to represent other insurance companies that compete with GEICO and its partners.

Is GEICO available only in the US?

While GEICO is primarily known for its operations in the United States, it does provide overseas insurance coverage through its affiliate, GEICO Financial Services GmbH. Since 1960, GEICO Financial Services GmbH has been acting as an agent and arranging coverage from reputable overseas insurers licensed by the respective countries where policyholders reside.
