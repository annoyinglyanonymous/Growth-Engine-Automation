## Potential Trucking Insurance Discounts

Many people don’t realize that there are specific vehicle insurance types for certain vehicles. For example, if you own a dump truck or other commercial vehicles and drive it on public roads, then be sure to explore general vehicle coverage and policies explicitly related to trucks like yours!

Commercial driver’s license discounts: Commercial driver’s licenses are a great way to save money on your insurance. If you or any of the people in your company have commercial driver’s licenses, then you or your drivers may be eligible for a discount and save approximately 25 percent.

Discounts on truck insurance for business experience: Several insurance companies may offer a business experience discount if you’ve been in business for at least three years.

## 8 Common Trucking Insurance Discounts

Personal-policy discounts: There are many discounts available to those who cover their personal vehicles with the same insurance company as their commercial ones.

Multi-business policies: By getting commercial vehicle insurance from the same provider that provides your business owners or general liability policy, you can save a significant amount of money. This is known as buying in a “package deal.”

Discount for paying your annual policy in full: You can save money by paying your yearly policy in full. You may be eligible for a 15% reduction on your commercial truck insurance.

Continuous insurance discount: You may be eligible for a discount if you have had continuous commercial vehicle insurance for a long time with the same carrier.

Discounts for loyal customers: In order to show their appreciation, some companies offer discounts or special deals exclusively for customers who have been insured with them for at least 12 months.

Discounts for Electronic Logging Devices (ELDs): If you utilize an ELD and give the insurance provider permission to see your driving data, you may be eligible to earn a reduction on your commercial truck insurance.

Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) discount: You may be eligible for a discount if you agree to pay your business insurance payments via an electronic funds transfer (EFT). For electronic payments, commercial vehicle insurance firms lower their prices. Payments are made straight from your checking account with an EFT. You may also be able to save money on check processing fees.

Increasing your deductibles: Choosing a higher deductible on your business vehicle insurance will help you save money quickly. You must set a deductible that you will be able to pay if you need to file a significant claim.

## Additional Ways to Save Money on Trucking Insurance

Recruit safe truck drivers: Because safe truck drivers are less expensive to insure, only hire drivers with a strong safety record. If you’re an owner-operator, make sure your driving record is spotless.

Maintain insurance regularly: Even if you run a seasonal business, you must maintain insurance regularly to get the best rates.

Get numerous quotes: To save money on truck insurance, you need to shop around. It’s crucial to remember that policies and companies differ significantly, so you’ll need to search everywhere to discover the best deals for your truck or fleet.

Hire experienced drivers: Rates are affected by driver experience as well. In general, the more years of driving experience your driver(s) has, the lower the insurance cost.

Take good care of your money: If you own a fleet, your financial situation or credit score may impact your insurance premiums.
