## What Does Minimum State Requirements Mean?

Minimum state requirements are the mandatory coverage limit requirements that business owners must fulfill for their commercial vehicles in each state in the United States.

Most states set minimum compulsory limits for coverage like bodily injury coverage, liability coverage, personal injury protection, and uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage. The states set the limits for each coverage by assessing the risks involved.

However, business owners must understand that the minimum state requirements are not always sufficient to cover all their commercial vehicle insurance requirements.

Therefore, they must consult with an insurance expert to add more coverage limits depending on their necessity. In short, the intention behind getting commercial auto insurance should not be to only get by the state laws but to get coverage that adequately shields your business during times of crisis.
