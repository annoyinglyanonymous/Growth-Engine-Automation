What kind of insurance do I need for commercial trucking?

Liability, Physical Damage, Cargo and General Liability are some specified coverage you need for trucking business.

Do truck drivers need separate insurance coverage?

Truck drivers usually need to have their own insurance policies that cover them for any accidents or damage that they may cause while driving. Cargo insurance is also a common requirement for truck drivers, as it covers any goods that are being transported and can become damaged or lost in transit.

## What Does Commercial Trucking Insurance Cover?

### General Liability Insurance

General liability insurance provides coverage for accidental damage, property damage, and medical bills associated with the accidents.

### Workers Compensation Insurance

Workers compensation insurance provides coverage as reimbursements for employees’ lost income, medical costs of employees, and rehabilitation expenses, among others. This coverage is designed in such a way that the employees are protected from being fired or having their wages reduced for reporting injuries on the job.

Commercial Umbrella Insurance

Commercial umbrella insurance provides coverage when your current insurance policy is not enough to cover large financial losses. Commercial umbrella insurance usually covers errors & omissions, and product liability claims for trucking businesses.

## Why is Commercial Trucking Insurance Important?

Commercial trucking insurance is designed for trucks that are used for business, farming, or other commercial purposes. If you use your truck for personal reasons, you won’t be covered under a commercial truck insurance policy.

For business operators, commercial trucking is a vital part of their operations. Without trucks to transport goods and materials, businesses would grind to a halt. Trucking companies play a vital role in keeping the wheels of commerce turning.

Trucking insurance is monumental for the smooth functioning of trucking businesses because there are a number of risks involved in commercial trucking, including: accidents, damage to goods being transported, theft, and liability. All of these risks can be covered by insurance policies designed specifically for commercial trucking businesses.
