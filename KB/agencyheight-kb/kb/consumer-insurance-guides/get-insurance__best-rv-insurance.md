## Easy Way to Find the Best RV Insurance

RVs, like any other type of vehicle, are not immune to accidents and damage. So, if you want to make sure that you and your mobile home are financially protected against any unfortunate event, finding proper RV insurance is a must.

Understand the basics of the best RV insurance policy and gain some insights to make the right decision while you find your RV insurance agent.

## Is RV Insurance a Viable Choice for You?

Ever since their first introduction in 1915, RVs have been the vehicle of choice for groups and individuals planning a long-distance journey on the road. Think about the many times you’ve tossed and turned in bed, wondering whether you’ll be okay if your RV is totaled. Having the security of a RV insurance not only saves your bank account but also relieves your sleepless nights.

RV insurance protects you and financially covers your RV from any unexpected out-of-pocket costs in the event of an accident during such journies.

If you get into an accident and end up damaging your vehicle or someone else’s vehicle or property, you will file a claim with your carrier. As long as the specific peril is covered, your RV insurance will financially compensate you up to your coverage limit.

## How Much Is RV Insurance Going to Cost You?

The exact cost of your RV insurance will vary widely based on which insurance company you get them from.

Generally, your RV insurance costs is dependent upon the class of the RV. The price range accodring to the classes are:

Class A: $1,000 to $1,300 annually

Class B: $500 to $1,000 annually

Class C: $800 to $1,000 annually

Is RV insurance a good idea?

If you buy RV insurance, you can kick back and relax knowing you’re protected in the case of an accident. Even if it may not be required by your state or lender, insuring your RV or camper is a great idea, significantly if you’ve invested a good chunk of change.

Which types of insurance are required for an RV?

Did you know that all states in the U.S.A, except New Hampshire and Virginia, require RV insurance according to the government? You will have to pay an uninsured motor vehicle fee even in such locations. You must have general liability insurance everywhere else, and in no-fault areas, you must also have personal injury protection. In these states, the RV insurance costs range from $800 to $2,000 each.

Is water damage covered by RV insurance?

Exclusions could apply, but comprehensive RV insurance packages can shield your RV against water damage. However, note that defects, water leaks, and general wear & tear are typically not covered, although each policy can be unique. Before enrolling in an RV insurance policy, talk with your agent about what is and isn’t covered.

## What Coverages Are Included In Your RV Insurance?

In most states, carrying liability insurance is mandatory if you’re driving an RV. The state minimum is generally the same as car insurance. However, you may need additional coverage due to RVs being specialty vehicles.

In general, RV insurance includes the following:

- Bodily Injury Liability

- Property Damage Liability

- Collision Coverage

- Comprehensive Coverage

- Uninsured And Underinsured Motorists

- Medical Payments

- Roadside Assistance

- Safety Glass Replacement

- Awning Replacement

- Vacation Liability
