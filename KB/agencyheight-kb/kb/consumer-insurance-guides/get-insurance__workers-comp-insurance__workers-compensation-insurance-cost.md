Workers Compensation Insurance provides health and wage benefits to people who are injured or sick on the job. Coverage is mandated by each state, and wages and medical benefits vary by state. Workers compensation insurance is considered social insurance because it is based on a social contract between employer and employee. Employers object to purchasing workers’ compensation insurance and are protected from civil claims by employees injured on the job.

However, there are limits to the benefits of each party. Workers Compensation Insurance is purchased by companies and underwritten by insurance companies. Also, in some states, publicly sponsored state funds undertake.

## How is the cost of workers’ compensation insurance determined?

Similar kind of businesses in each state with comparable workplace injury patterns and costs are grouped into “classes.” A rate is set for each category based on the last 5 years of cost of loss for all firms within that class. This provides an equitable system for calculating rates that reflect the industry’s actual loss experience. The economic aspects for each state are then considered to determine rates for each class in a given state.

Experience rating system allows class fees to be modified based on the loss history of individual companies. This system gives entrepreneurs greater control over the cost of workers’ accident insurance premiums. Safe companies have lower premiums, and unsafe companies have higher premiums.

## Average Cost of Workers’ Compensation Insurance

The average cost of workers’ compensation insurance is $936 per employee per year, or $78 per month. Workers Comp insurance premium rates vary greatly depending on the state, type of business, years of service, and the number of workers’ compensation claims the company has received. Overall, workers’ compensation premiums account for 1.2% of the total cost of hiring the average American worker. This estimate is based on wage and bonus data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics.

## How are workers' comp premiums calculated?

Workers compensation insurance costs are evaluated according to the worker’s classification and the rate assigned to each worker’s classification. The premium rate itself is expressed in dollars and cents per $100 salary for each class code. In most states, the National Council on Compensation Insurance (NCCI) determines classification rates and experience modifiers (MOD).

Cost for workers’ compensation insurance is calculated by the formula below:

Payroll (per $100) X Class Code Rate X Experience Modifier (if applicable) + State Taxes & Fees = Premium

## Experience Modification Rating Factor

The Experience Modification Rating Factor (EMR) is a rating factor that typically starts at 0.70 and increases to over 2.00. This valuation factor is applied to premiums generated by class rates and salaries. An example is shown below.

Payroll x Rate = Premium

$1,000,000 10.00 $100,000

If the EMR is 0.70, then your premium becomes:

$1,000,000 x 10.00 = $100,000 x 0.70 = $70,000

Alternatively, If the EMR is 1.50, then your premium becomes:

$1,000,000 x 10.00 = $100,000 x 1.50 = $150,000

All this assumes that other evaluation factors remain the same. However, if the EMR is high, it is the result of significant claims activity and may not qualify you from preferential workers compensation rates, resulting in even higher premiums.

What is the workers comp rate in Georgia?

Georgia’s worker’s comp index is $1.64. That means you pay an average of $1.64 for every $100 in payroll. If a person has three employees with a total salary of $100,000, they will pay about $1,640 as worker’scompensation per year.

Is workers comp insurance required in Georgia?

By law, all companies with three or more employees, including regular part-time workers, must have worker’s compensation insurance.

How to get workers comp insurance in Georgia?

The Georgia Workers Compensation Board does not sell workers compensation insurance. An insurance agent or broker will help you get an affordable and reliable workers comp insurance in Georgia.
