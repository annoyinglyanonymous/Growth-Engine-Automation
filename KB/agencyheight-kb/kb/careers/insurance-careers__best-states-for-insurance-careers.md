With a plethora of employment opportunities across the country, it can be challenging to choose the best state to work in. To help you find the ideal state for your insurance career, we’ve created this list of the top 3 states to pursue your insurance career in.

## Insurance Careers In Florida

The vast majority of the state’s population lives in a coastal county in Florida, famed for its beaches that stretch along more than 1,300 miles of coastline.

In addition to its many beaches, Florida is among the states with the highest concentration of insurance sales agents. The Sunshine State employs 36,230 insurance sales representatives.

Florida’s economy, which had a $1.2 trillion gross state product (GSP) as of 2021 and was the fourth-largest in the U.S., offers its citizens a wide range of employment options, including in the insurance industry.

### Best Insurance Companies To Work With In Florida

With the rise in people looking for insurance, Florida is also home to some of the best insurance companies in the country. Here are some of the best insurance firms to work with in Florida:

Bankers Life Insurance

With around 4,400 career agents spread throughout 260 U.S. sales locations, Bankers Life has one of the largest agent forces in the country.

The staff at Bankers Life Insurance come from diverse demographic backgrounds. While the company has high demographic diversity, employees at Bankers Life Insurance tend to have a relatively short tenure. Staff members usually stay with the company for 2.6 years.

Tower Hill

Tower Hill has developed into one of Florida’s largest and most recognized commercial and residential property insurance providers since its founding in 1972. Over the years, Tower Hill has been trusted by over 1.7 million customers to protect their residences and places of business.

As a financially solid company that offers considerable opportunities, Tower Hill is an excellent company for career advancement that provides competitive benefits to its employees.

Bristol West

With 1,154 employees and $375M in yearly sales, the Florida-based Bristol West Insurance Group is a significant player in the insurance sector. Bristol West Holdings is a prominent supplier of non-standard private passenger automobile insurance and related services.

Based on more than 133 anonymous employee evaluations, Bristol West Insurance Group has a glassdoor user rating of 3.5 out of 5. Employee satisfaction at Bristol West Insurance Group is 63%, and 58% of workers would recommend the company to a friend.

## Insurance Careers In Georgia

Since its establishment, Georgia has always been a state in transition. Across organizations throughout the state, there are a variety of professional possibilities available. Georgia remains a forward-thinking and wealthy state, home to thousands of individuals in insurance careers.

With over 18,650 insurance sales agents employed, Georgia ranks among the top 5 highest paying states for insurance sales agents. Insurance agents in Georgia typically make $40.7 per hour and $83,760 per year on average.

### Best Insurance Companies To Work With In Georgia

It’s always worthwhile to consider pursuing your insurance career in Georgia, where one of the highest-paying insurance jobs is accessible. Some of the best insurance companies to work for in Georgia are listed below:

Aflac

The team at Aflac comes from an incredibly broad demographic background, and the organization has excellent employee retention, with employees staying for an average of 3.6 years.

Established in 1995, Aflac is dedicated to fostering a culture that values an atmosphere of compassion and empathy. Ranked among the “100 Best Companies to Work For” in Fortune Magazine for the past 17 years, Aflac claims to provide a welcoming workplace with a corporate culture that values a transparent management style, competitive pay, and top-notch benefits.

Georgia Farm Bureau

Georgia Farm Bureau Mutual Insurance Company, headquartered in Georgia, is a significant player in the insurance sector.

Established in 1937, Georgia Farm Bureau provides a healthy office atmosphere with welcoming employees. Agents for Georgia Farm Bureau Insurance offer banking services, life insurance, farm insurance, house insurance, and auto insurance. The average employee tenure at Georgia Farm Bureau Mutual Insurance Company is 6.3 years, demonstrating the company’s excellent employee retention.

Transamerica

The Transamerica Corporation is an American holding corporation for numerous life insurance businesses and investment organizations offering life and additional health insurance, investments, investments, and retirement services.

Transamerica was honored by the Diversity Best Practices Inclusion Index, the Working Mother 100 Best Companies, and the Human Rights Campaign Foundation’s Corporate Equality Index for its commitment to fostering a safe and inviting workplace for all employees. The organization enjoys strong employee retention with an average tenure of 4.9 years.

## Insurance Careers In Texas

For the first time since before the epidemic, Texas recorded more than 13 million jobs in December 2021. According to labor economist and the former University of Texas at Austin economics professor Daniel Hamermesh, Texas is the state with the highest population growth in the nation, and as a result, there is a greater need for workers.

With the rise in people seeking out professions in insurance in Texas, there are 33,010 insurance sales employed in the state as of 2022.

### Best Insurance Companies To Work With In Texas

The insurance sector in Texas can provide you with a fulfilling work environment that promotes both your professional and personal growth. Among the best insurance firms in Texas to work for are some of the followings:

HealthMarkets

HealthMarkets is a medium-sized insurance provider headquartered in Texas. The employees at HealthMarkets come from an extensive range of demographic backgrounds, all with the goal of assisting consumers in making knowledgeable decisions regarding insurance products that can help safeguard their health and financial security. Women make up 48.3 % of the workforce, and people of color make up 41.0 %.

HealthMarkets aims to help you succeed as an individual in the insurance industry, whether you’re a seasoned insurance agent or an entrepreneurial spirit seeking a new career path.

American National Insurance

Headquartered in Galveston, Texas, American National insurance is a significant company in the insurance industry. The company and its affiliates operate in all 50 states and Puerto Rico.

The demographic backgrounds of the employees at American National Insurance are particularly diversified. With an average tenure of 4.3 years, the organization enjoys excellent employee retention. With 4,621 workers and a Texas base, American National Insurance aims to establish a work atmosphere that encourages and rewards ambitious employees. American National Insurance, established in 1905, was ranked among the top financial institutions for trustworthiness in 2017 by Forbes Magazine.

Argo Group

Argo Group International Holdings, Ltd., also known as Argo Group, is an international underwriter of specialty insurance and reinsurance products in the property and liability market with its headquarters in Bermuda. Since 1957, the business has worked with independent agents, wholesale brokers, and retail brokerage partners to provide the best products for specific markets.

The demographic backgrounds of the Argo Group employees are remarkably diversified. The average employee tenure at the organization is 3.5 years, which demonstrates excellent employee retention. As of 2020, the San Antonio, Texas-based Argo Group has 1,515 workers and generated $1.89 billion in yearly revenue.
