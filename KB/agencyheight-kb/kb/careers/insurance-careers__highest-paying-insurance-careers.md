Read More About Insurance Broker Fee vs Commission

For most people, especially for experienced agents, commissions are the most typical source of pay. The kind, quantity, and whether the transaction involves a new policy or a renewal affect how much of a commission is earned. Agents typically receive incentives when they surpass their sales objectives or when the agency achieves its financial targets. A charge rather than a commission is sometimes paid to some financial planning agents.

Following client meetings during business hours, some salespeople spend the evenings completing paperwork and putting together presentations for potential customers. The majority of agents work full-time, and some put in over 40 hours every week.

Financial advisors help clients manage wealth, investments, and insurance products.

Financial services companies that employ personal financial advisors often pay them a salary plus bonuses. The wage data presented here includes commissions, incentive pay, and production incentives but excludes nonproduction bonuses.

## Claims adjusters, Appraisers, Examiners, and Investigators

The typical pay range for a claim’s examiner, insurance adjusters,or investigator depends on experience and education.

These professionals evaluate claims, assess damages, and investigate cases for insurance companies.

Salary Range (2023): $41,950 – $103,610

Average Annual Salary: Around $75,000

In the major industries where they worked, claims adjusters, examiners, and investigators earned the following median annual salaries in May 2025:

Federal government

$84,850

Direct insurance (except life, health, and medical) carriers

$75,700

State government, excluding education and hospitals

$68,530

Agencies, brokerages, and other insurance related activities

$74,560

Direct health and medical insurance carriers

$75,350

The majority of claim adjusters, valuers, examiners, and investigators have full-time jobs. Their work schedules, though, might differ.

In order to accommodate client appointments on the weekends and in the evenings, adjusters frequently modify their work schedules. Adjusters occasionally work irregular schedules as a result of this obligation, particularly when they have a large number of claims to examine.

Because they need to get in touch with people who are unavailable during typical business hours, insurance investigators frequently work odd hours. Weekend and overnight work are frequently required.

Auto damage appraisers, on the other hand, normally work regular hours and don’t often work on the weekends.

## Highest Paying Insurance Companies in 2026: Best Insurance Companies to Work For

If you are looking for a job in the insurance industry, you may wonder which companies offer the highest salary and best benefits. To help narrow your search, here’s a compiled list of the top insurance companies to work for in terms of compensation and employee satisfaction. Among them are GEICO, AIG Life & Retirement, Prudential Financial, MetLife, and State Farm Insurance.

### GEICO

One of the top-paying insurance companies to work for, GEICO offers competitive salaries and benefits. They offer a wide range of job opportunities, from customer service representatives to underwriters. An insurance agent in GEICO earns an average of $37500 to $55000 per year.

### AIG Life & Retirement

This company is also one of the best insurance companies to work for, offering competitive salaries and benefits. They provide various job opportunities, from customer service representatives to underwriters. An insurance agent in AIG earns an average of $47000 to $68000 per year.

### Prudential Financial

Prudential Financial is another top insurance company to work for and provides competitive salaries and benefits packages. Prudential Financial is popular for its flexible working hours, which can be a great perk for employees. An insurance agent at Prudential Financial can earn an average of $64000 to $82000 per year.

### MetLife

One of the top-paying insurance companies to work for, MetLife provides excellent benefits and competitive salaries. They offer various job opportunities, from customer service representatives to underwriters. An insurance agent in MetLife earns an average of $78000 to $94000 annually.

### State Farm Insurance

State Farm Insurance is another top company to work for and provides competitive salaries and benefits. An insurance agent in State Farm earns an average of $56000 to $78000 per year. They also offer various job opportunities, from customer service representatives to underwriters.

## Why Choose a Career in Insurance

There are several reasons why a career in insurance can be rewarding. Here are a few primary ones:

Provides a sense of purpose

At its core, the insurance industry is all about safeguarding members of society. Imagine being the reason a family can rebuild their home after a devastating fire or giving peace of mind to loved ones after a tragic loss. Moreover, insurance also steps in to support injured workers. By promoting the sharing of risk, insurance ensures that everyone is protected when life takes an unexpected turn.

Job Security

According to the most recent 2024–2034 BLS projections, the insurance industry remains a bedrock of the economy. While specific roles vary, Insurance Sales Agents are projected to grow by 4%, and Actuaries are exploding at a 22% growth rate—far exceeding the national average. Overall, the industry is expected to add over 47,000 openings annually to account for both growth and the ‘Great Retirement’ of senior professionals. They don’t call insurance an evergreen career for nothing. While nothing is certain in life, insurance provides comparatively more security than many jobs out there.

Opportunities to widen your skill set

With endless possibilities and a demand for diverse skill sets, this booming field offers a multitude of career paths.

Communication, technology, problem-solving, research, analytics, and organization – these are the sought-after skills that insurance companies value. But it doesn’t stop there. In a world that’s constantly evolving, employers in the insurance industry also seek individuals well-versed in social media, digital marketing, and cyber communication. There is so much to learn, and so many different skills set you can touch by entering this field.

Easy to get started

With just a high school diploma or equivalent, you can jumpstart your journey as an insurance agent, even without any prior work experience. In fact, you could be on your way in a matter of weeks! Don’t forget to pick your specialty and obtain the necessary license.

Is insurance a growing industry?
