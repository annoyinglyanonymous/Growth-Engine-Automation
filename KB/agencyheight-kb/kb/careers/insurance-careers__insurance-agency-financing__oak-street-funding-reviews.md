## How Oak Street Funding Is Different from Traditional Lenders?

Oak Street Funding stands out from traditional lenders by offering financing that’s specifically designed for professionals in industries like insurance, accounting, and financial services. Instead of requiring personal assets as collateral, they base loans on future revenue. It makes it easier for business owners to get the funding they need without putting their personal finances at risk. This approach is especially helpful for those looking to grow, acquire another business, or plan for succession.

Unlike banks, which often rely on rigid credit requirements and slow approval processes, Oak Street Funding takes a more flexible route. Their in-house team works closely with clients to offer faster turnaround times and customized loan structures. For businesses that don’t fit the typical banking mold, Oak Street provides a practical and accessible path to capital.
