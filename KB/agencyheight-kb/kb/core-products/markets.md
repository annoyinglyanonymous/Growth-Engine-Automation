FAQs

Questions about Agency Height Markets

Can I write insurance policies without a carrier appointment?

Yes. Submit the risk through Agency Height Markets and our underwriting team places it with a select wholesale carrier. You earn the commission, we handle the carrier relationship. No appointment needed, no new portal, no separate tool. Works from day one for any agent.

What is an insurance carrier appointment and how do I get one through Agency Height Markets?

A carrier appointment is authorization from an insurance company to sell its policies and earn commission. In Agency Height Markets, request an appointment from the carrier card. Your NPN, E&O, W9, and state license details pre-fill from your profile. Apply through the carrier's partner portal using our link, or submit to our team for manual appointments. Most appointments confirm within 48 hours.

How do I get appointed as a new agent with no production history?

Submit risks to our underwriting team from day one. Select carriers require no volume minimums, and the premium you place builds the production history carriers need to appoint you directly.

What is an MGA and what is E&S insurance?

An MGA (Managing General Agent) is a wholesale intermediary with underwriting authority. They specialize in risks standard carriers won’t write, like niche commercial, specialty property, or professional liability. E&S (Excess & Surplus Lines) carriers write risks admitted carriers won’t: coastal property, contractors, businesses with prior losses, and more. Agency Height Markets shows E&S carriers in the same search as admitted carriers, so you never have to search multiple places when a standard market declines.

How is Agency Height Markets different from First Connect or Smart Choice?

They solve carrier access through different models. Smart Choice is an agency network: a territory manager coordinates your appointments under their master code, and you share commission on business written through their carrier partners. First Connect is a carrier access platform that routes small commercial E&S through a partner wholesaler. Agency Height Markets puts admitted and E&S markets in one self-serve search, adds direct submission with no appointment required, and connects to your CRM, leads, and website inside Agency Height rather than living in a separate portal.

What does insurance market access mean for independent agents?

Market access is your ability to represent carriers and place policies. Without enough of it, you turn away clients when standard markets decline a risk. Agency Height Markets expands your access across admitted and E&S markets, without requiring you to build carrier relationships one by one or maintain separate appointments in each market.
