FAQs

Questions agents ask before signing up.

Do I get carrier access, or just leads?

Both. Markets gives you carrier access, search carriers by appetite, request access in a click, and submit to some wholesale carriers with no appointment needed. And beyond Markets, tools like profile listing, intake forms, and Lead Bank keep leads coming in. AH Markets

How do insurance agents get leads on Agency Height?

Four paths. (1) Your public profile: a buyer finds your listing and submits a request straight to you, exclusive. (2) Your campaign link: share your form anywhere, every submission is yours alone. (3) Platform matching: a buyer picks from up to 3 matched agents, so the same lead can go to up to 3 of you. (4) Lead Bank: unclaimed requests you claim by state and line, exclusive once claimed, capped by plan (Premium 2/mo, Enterprise 7/mo). On Free, paths 1 to 3 still arrive, but you can open full details on only 2 leads before upgrading.

What is insurance agent lead generation through a directory?

Buyers find you through search instead of you chasing them. Someone searches for a licensed agent in their state, sees your verified profile, and submits a request. No cold calling, no bought list. Agency Height matches by licensed state, line, and profile quality.

How does my insurance agent profile affect my ranking in the directory?

State and line match come first. Among agents who pass, ranking weighs NPN verification, profile completeness, plan tier, recent activity, response speed, and ratings. A verified, complete, fast-replying profile on a paid plan outranks a dormant or incomplete one.

Is Agency Height free for insurance agents?

Yes. Create a profile, get NPN verified, and start receiving leads free. Free doesn't block leads, it limits how many you can open in full: after 2, you upgrade to see the rest. Lead Bank claims and the Recommended badge need a paid plan, Premium ($29.99/mo) or Enterprise ($79.99/mo), billed annually.

How is this different from buying leads from QuoteWizard or EverQuote?

They sell leads in bulk to several agents at once, so you pay per lead and compete with up to 3 or 4 others who got the same contact. Here, a buyer finds your profile and chooses you, and that request is yours exclusively. Lead Bank claims are exclusive too, one agent per lead.

What lines of insurance are included in the directory?

Personal lines (auto, home, renters, umbrella), Commercial, and Life. Health isn't included. Your coverage types come from your NPN license data, so only lines you're licensed for show publicly.

How does insurance agent SEO work with my profile?

Your profile is a standalone, indexable page, with your name and location in the URL and your states and coverage in the content. That helps it rank when buyers search for a local agent. A fuller profile with reviews ranks better on Agency Height and in search.

Can I share my profile on social media and business cards?

Yes. Share your profile URL and Lead Form link anywhere, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, your Google Business Profile, business cards, or a QR code. Every submission lands in the same My Leads inbox as your matched leads.
