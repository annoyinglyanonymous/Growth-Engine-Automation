## What is Medical Malpractice Insurance for General Surgeons?

Medical malpractice insurance protects you if a patient files a lawsuit claiming negligence, error, or omission. Without coverage, you’re personally liable for hundreds of thousands of dollars—even if no wrongdoing occurred. It covers:

###### Legal defense fees

###### Settlements and awarded damages

###### Costs even if the claim is dropped

## The Risks You Face Every Day

General surgeons face some of the highest malpractice risks in medicine, making liability insurance essential. Every day, complex procedures carry out the potential for serious complications. Intraoperative errors like wrong-site surgery or retained tools are major causes of claims. According to covers study, 15% of general surgery claims involve patient death, and nearly 1 in 10 involve retained objects.

Risks don’t end in the OR. Postoperative complications, infections, or diagnostic delays can trigger lawsuits. JAMA reports General surgeons face a paid-claim rate of 30 per 1,000 physician-year, ranking them among the most-sued doctors.

Without proper coverage, you risk:

- A routine surgery turning into a negligence claim

- Soaring legal defense costs

- Loss of hospital privileges or licensing issue

## How to Choose the Right Policy as
a General Surgeon

Selecting the right malpractice policy is crucial to protect your career and practice. Focus on these key factors:

#### Carrier Experience

Choose an insurer with a strong track record defending general surgeons and financial stability.

#### Policy Limits & Type

Ensure coverage limits (e.g., $1M per claim / $3M aggregate) are sufficient and understand claims‑made vs. occurrence policies.

#### Tail & Retroactive Coverage

Verify coverage for past work if you change jobs or states.

#### State Approval & Financial Strength

Confirm the insurer is licensed in your state and can handle large claims.

#### Risk Management Support

Look for carriers offering training, surgical checklists, and documentation to reduce claims and premiums.

Questions to Ask Your Broker:

- “Does this policy cover off-site or multi-state work?”

- “Who pays for tail coverage if I leave?”

- “What risk-management programs or discounts are available?”

Choosing carefully ensures your policy aligns with the real risks of general surgery, keeping your practice and reputation protected.
