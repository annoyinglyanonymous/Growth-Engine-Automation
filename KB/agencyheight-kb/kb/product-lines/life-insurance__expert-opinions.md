Willing to truthfully answer the health questions. Additionally, have legal identification to prove you are insured. Have your banking information available to set up the monthly premium.

How are the needs of the life insurance buyer evolving? Could you share a scenario where you went the extra mile to fulfill a unique insurance need?

What advice would you like to give a beneficiary to do on their end to make the claim process go more smoothly?

Please make sure to inform the insurer of the policy about any changes such as your name, address, and phone number to avoid any processing delays.

### Michael L. Parish LUTCF

What suggestions would you give to someone who is new to the insurance process and is thinking of getting a life insurance policy?

First, you need to ask yourself what your purpose for buying life insurance is and what do you want it to accomplish. Is it for providing income for your family, paying the mortgage, covering burial expenses, etc.? What other resources do you have that would help in providing for the aforementioned situations. Sit down with your loved ones to discuss and make written plans to address the need for life insurance. There can be many things to consider and having a trusted advisor to assist can be very beneficial.

How are the needs of the life insurance buyer evolving? Could you share a scenario where you went the extra mile to fulfill a unique insurance need?

As an insurance agent for many years I have seen firsthand how having adequate life insurance will help you. It will keep the utilities paid, the mortgage paid in the event of the loss of life of the family’s bread winner. I have seen life insurance pay for the education of many children. One thing is unchanging: Life insurance is the only means to create an immediate estate.

What is some valuable advice would you like to offer a beneficiary to do on their end to make the claim process go more smoothly and faster?

Make sure all the information concerning your claim is accurate and readily available to submit to your company. For example, for a life insurance claim, you would need the policy information like policy numbers and beneficiary info, death certificate and attending physician’s statement, etc. For an auto-claim you would need the police report. You also need policy information and preferably a company provided phone no. or app to send pictures of damage and other claims reporting documentation.

### Syd Rogers

What documents are necessary while buying a life policy? What is some advice would you offer someone new to the life insurance process?

Have your Driver’s License info. Current height and weight, Medications taken with dosage levels, Doctors seen in last 3 years with their office address and telephone number. Additionally, the name/s of your beneficiary/beneficiaries along with their Social Security number/s and date/s of birth.

Are the needs of life insurance buyers evolving? Could you share a scenario where you went the extra mile to fulfill a unique insurance need?

With inflation on the rise, many people are buying low cost, highly competitive Term Life Insurance from Independent Agents like me. Due to my 30 years’ experience and large volume of Term life bought from me by phone. I have often been able to lower the original price quote by interfering directly with company Underwriters.

What are the things that a beneficiary to do on their end to make the insurance claim process go more smoothly?

Be sure to obtain a certified Death Certificate, and verifiable proof of your identity as the named beneficiary in the policy.

### Miguel Clark

As a life insurance agent, what advice would you like to offer to first time insurance buyers about preparing the documents necessary to get a policy?

As a broker, it is suggested to have an accessible list of financial documents and a clear understanding of your requirements.

How has the needs of life insurance buyers changed over the years? Could you provide us with a scenario where you worked to fulfill their unique insurance need?

Every individual, a family, has unique needs and goals, with inflation families need more coverage and the ability to protect their assets and family. Having dozens of carriers and products has allowed me to find programs that help every unique situation. Many of the clients and families I have helped were told they had a two-year waiting period or given a higher price because they were placed in the incorrect risk class.
