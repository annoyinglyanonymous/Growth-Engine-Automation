## Key Coverage Types in Garage Insurance

Garage insurance is made up of several specialized coverages to protect your business from day-to-day risks and unexpected events. Understanding each type ensures you’re not left exposed to costly claims or gaps in protection.

#### Garage Liability Insurance

Covers injuries or property damage to third parties caused by your business operations. For example, if a customer slips in your shop or a vehicle causes damage while being moved, this coverage handles legal fees and settlements.

#### Garagekeepers Legal Liability Insurance

Protects customer vehicles while in your care, custody, or control. Whether a car is stolen, damaged by fire, or involved in a collision at your facility, this coverage ensures your business can cover repairs and maintain client trust.

#### Commercial Auto Insurance

Covers vehicles owned or operated by your business, like tow trucks, shuttles, or courtesy cars. This ensures accidents, damage, or liability during transport are financially covered, keeping your operations running smoothly.

#### Garage property Insurance

Protects your building, equipment, tools, lifts, and inventory from fire, theft, vandalism, or natural disasters. This safeguards your investments and minimizes downtime if a loss occurs.

#### Workers’ Compensation Insurance

Covers medical bills and lost wages for employees injured on the job. This protects both your team and your business from lawsuits or unexpected expenses due to workplace injuries

#### Umbrella / Excess Liability Insurance

Provides an extra layer of protection above your primary policies, covering claims that exceed your standard limits. This coverage protects your business assets in case of catastrophic incidents, like multi-vehicle accidents or severe injuries, giving you peace of mind without significantly increasing premiums.

#### Optional Coverages

Add-ons like Dealers E&O, business interruption, tool/equipment floater, and hired/non-owned auto liability fill coverage gaps. These options ensure your garage is fully protected against a wide range of risks.

## Factors That Affect Garage Insurance Costs

Your premium depends on the level of risk and coverage you choose. Knowing these factors helps you balance cost with protection.

###### Type of Business

Repair shops, body shops, and dealerships carry different risk profiles.

###### Number of Vehicles in Custody

More vehicles handled daily increases exposure

###### Location

Urban areas often have higher premiums due to greater accident or theft risk.

###### Size & Revenue

Larger operations with more employees face greater liability.

###### Claims History

Prior claims or losses can increase premiums.

###### Coverage Limits & Deductibles

Higher limits provide more protection but cost more.

Example: Consider two repair shops of the same size and services, one with a $28,000 recent claim and one without any claims. The shop with the claim can expect to pay higher premiums due to increased risk.

#### Typical U.S. Cost Ranges:

- Small shops: $1,000–$3,000/year

- Medium shops: $3,000–$8,000/year

- Large dealerships or multi-location garages: $10,000–$15,000+ per year

## How to Choose the Right Garage Insurance Policy

Selecting the right garage insurance means understanding your unique risks and carefully evaluating your options. The goal is to protect your vehicles, employees, and customers while avoiding unnecessary costs. A well-chosen policy ensures your business stays secure, compliant, and financially protected.

#### Understand Your Risks

Assess the specific operations of your garage, such as vehicle repair, storage, towing, or valet services. Knowing your exposure helps you choose the coverage types and limits that truly match your business needs.

#### Compare Providers

Don’t settle for the first quote. Compare multiple insurers to evaluate pricing, coverage options, and customer service. This ensures you find a reliable provider who can handle claims efficiently and provide long-term support

#### Check Policy Exclusions

Read the fine print carefully to identifywhat’s not covered. Exclusions can leave gaps in protection, so understanding them helps you avoid surprises and ensures comprehensive coverage for your garage operations.

#### Bundle Policies

Many insurers offer discounts when you combine coverage types, such as liability, commercial auto, and property insurance. Bundling can save money while simplifying the management of your policies.

#### Confirm Compliance

Ensure your policy meets all state requirements and any contractual obligations with clients or leasing companies. Compliance keeps your business legally protected and avoids penalties or gaps in coverage
