## Key Considerations When Selecting Environmental Liability Insurance

#### Coverage Scope and Limitations

Review policy definitions of “pollution conditions” and cover “cleanup costs” carefully. Ensure the policy addresses your specific operational exposures, including non-owned disposal sites and transportation risks.

#### Historical Coverage Needs

Determine whether your business requires coverage for pre-existing conditions or strictly for new pollution events. Legacy contamination coverage often involves additional underwriting scrutiny and premium considerations.

#### Regulatory Compliance Alignment

Ensure your coverage meets applicable regulatory requirements, including financial responsibility provisions for specific operations like underground storage tanks or hazardous waste facilities.

#### Claims Response Capabilities

Evaluate the insurer’s environmental claims handling expertise, response protocols, and access to qualified remediation contractors and legal specialists who understand environmental regulations.
