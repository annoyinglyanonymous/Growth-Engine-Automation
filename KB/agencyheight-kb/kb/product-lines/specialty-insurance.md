## Why Your Business Might be Hard-to-Place Risk?

Certain industries are classified as hard-to-place risks due to unique challenges and high levels of risk that insurers struggle to assess and mitigate. These sectors often face uncommon threats that make coverage difficult to secure. Here are the key reasons your business might fall under this category:

#### High Risk of Loss

Industries like construction, trucking, mining, and chemicals are high-risk due to accidents, property damage, or injuries. Businesses working with hazardous materials like oil or gas find it harder to secure coverage because of environmental risks. High-value properties in disaster-prone areas also make insurance tough to get.

#### High Volume of Previous Claims in the Industry

If any industry has high volume past claims, insurers may be hesitant to provide coverage due to the risky nature of the business. Industries like healthcare with high medical malpractice claims or commercial trucking with frequent claims get categorized as hard-to-place risks.

#### Complex Operations

Industries operating under complex operations or specialized processes also present challenge to insurers in assessing the adequacy of risks and estimating the losses. Industries such as aerospace, marine, high-manufacturing fall under this risk category.

#### Legal Challenges & Economic Volatility

Industries like pharmaceuticals, finance, and construction face legal hurdles such as compliance violations and fines, which can make coverage difficult. Similarly, industries like real estate, commodities, and construction often struggle to get insurance because of economic volatility and financial instability.

What is Specialty insurance?

Insurance Risks that are hard-to-place or hard to get coverage for due to high risk nature due to physical hazards, natural calamities, location, huge number of threats involved in the business which are usually unacceptable to the standard markets are categorized under hard-to-place insurance.

What are the examples of industries with hard-to-place insurance risks?

Some of the industries that are considered high risks by carriers are within transportation, healthcare, hospitality, high-value property & home, environmental, retail, technology, artisan & specialty classes areas.

Why some industries are classified as hard-to-place risk industries?

Industries that involve high risks, uncommon coverage needs, high volume of previous claims history, exposed or prone to natural disasters, high crime rates, volatile regulatory or market conditions get classified as hard-to-place insurance risks and often involves working with specialized brokers who can navigate these challenges to find appropriate coverage.
