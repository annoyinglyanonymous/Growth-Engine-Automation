## Types of Car Insurance Coverage

#### Liability Coverage

What it covers: Pays for injuries and property damage to others when you’re at fault. Also helps with legal defense if you’re sued.

Why you need it: It’s required by law in most states and protects you from paying massive out-of-pocket costs that could easily drain your savings after an accident.

#### Collision Coverage

What it covers: Collision coverage helps repair or replace your car if it’s damaged in a crash with another vehicle or object, or if it rolls over.

Why you need it: Essential for newer or financed cars, protects you from costly repairs and gets you back on the road fast.

#### Comprehensive Coverage

What it covers: Comprehensive coverage that protects your car from theft, fire, vandalism, falling objects, floods, storms, and animal collisions. It covers non-collision damages often excluded from basic insurance plans.

Why you need it: Disasters and theft are unpredictable. Having this coverage ensures your car is protected from unexpected risks beyond your control.

#### Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist Coverage

What it covers: This coverage pays for medical expenses and damages i if the at-fault driver has no insurance or too little coverage to fully pay damages.

Why you need it: Many drivers carry only the minimum coverage, or none. This coverage ensures that you and your passengers are financially protected when others fail to take responsibility.

#### Personal Injury Protection (PIP)

What it covers: PIP helps cover medical bills for you and your passengers after an accident, regardless of who is at fault.

Why you need it: PIP provides quick access to funds for recovery, removing delays caused by insurance disputes. It reduces financial stress so you can focus on healing after an accident.

#### Loan or Lease Gap Insurance

What it covers: Gap insurance covers the difference between your car’s actual cash value and the remaining balance on your loan or lease if it’s totaled.

Why you need it: Cars depreciate fast. Without gap insurance, you could owe thousands of vehicles you no longer have.

## Ways to Lower Car Insurance Costs

Car insurance doesn’t have to break the bank. With a few simple steps and informed choices, you can reduce your premiums while keeping the coverage you need.

###### Bundle policies

Bundle home and auto insurance with one provider to earn significant premium discounts.

###### Maintain a clean driving record

Drive safely and avoid violations to earn insurers’ trust and enjoy lower, long-term premium rates.

###### Increase your deductible

Opting for a higher deductible reduces your monthly premium while keeping essential accident coverage.

###### Install safety and anti-theft devices

Cars with alarms or tracking devices are harder to steal, leading insurers to lower your premium.

###### Take advantage of discounts

Discounts for good students, low mileage, or safe driving courses can lower premiums without cutting coverage.

###### Shop around for quotes

Regularly compare insurers’ offers, rates vary, and switching can reduce premiums without losing coverage.

###### Drive less

Driving fewer miles lowers accident risk, and many insurers offer low-mileage discounts for careful drivers.

###### Review coverage annually

Life changes, new cars, different commutes, safer neighborhood. Reviewing your policy yearly ensures you’re not overpaid for coverage you no longer need.

## What are Some Exclusions for Your Car Insurance?

Car insurance doesn’t cover everything. Common exclusions include intentional damage, racing, using your car for commercial purposes, wear and tear, and certain natural disasters if not included in your policy. Understanding these limits helps you avoid surprises and ensures you choose the right coverage for your specific needs, keeping you fully protected on the road.
