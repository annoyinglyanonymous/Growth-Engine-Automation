## Common Types of Business Insurance

##### General Liability Insurance

Covers your business if someone sues for injury, property damage, or advertising-related claims.

Who it’s for: Companies that interact with customers, work on client property, or advertise services.

##### Workers’ Compensation Insurance

Required in most states, it covers medical bills, lost wages, and rehabilitation for employees injured or made ill on the job.

Who it’s for: Any business with part-time or full-time employees.

##### Professional Liability Insurance

Also called errors and omissions insurance, this protects your business if a client claims financial loss due to your professional services or advice, even if no error occurred.

Who it’s for: Consultants, accountants, architects, lawyers, and service professionals.

##### Commercial Property Insurance

Protects buildings, equipment, tools, and inventory against damage or loss from events like fire, theft, or natural disasters.
Who it’s for: Businesses that own or rent property or rely on equipment to operate.

##### Commercial Auto Insurance

Covers vehicles used for business if they are involved in accidents, whether owned, leased, or personal but used for work purposes.

Who it’s for: Businesses that use vehicles for deliveries, client visits, or employee transportation.

##### Cyber Insurance

Protects against data breaches, cyberattacks, and other technology-related risks, including legal fees and recovery costs.

Who it’s for: Businesses that operate online, handle sensitive information, or maintain a network.

## Business Insurance Checklist

#### Risk Assessment

Identify potential risks to your business, employees, and clients to understand where protection is most needed.

#### Legal Requirements

Confirm which coverage is required by your state, such as workers’ compensation or professional liability.

#### Asset Protection

Evaluate your property, equipment, and vehicles to ensure all critical business assets are covered.

#### Professional Coverage

Assess professional liability risks for the services or advice your business provides to clients.

#### Cyber Protection

Include cyber liability coverage if your business handles sensitive data, operates online, or manages a network.

#### Policy Bundling

Consider a Business Owner’s Policy (BOP) or other bundled coverages to simplify protection and potentially save premiums.
