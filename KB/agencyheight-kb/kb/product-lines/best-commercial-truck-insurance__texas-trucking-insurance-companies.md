## Texas Trucking Insurance Overview

Trucking companies, known as motor carriers, are required by Texas law to file proof of commercial automobile liability insurance for each registered vehicle. The insurance requirements for truck drivers and trucking companies ensure they are covered in the event of a serious accident or injury.

## Mandatory Insurance Coverages for Texas-Based Trucking Businesses

- Primary Liability: Texas law mandated all commercial trucks to have primary liability insurance.

- Trucker’s Legal Liability Insurance: This is mandatory in Texas if the business is transporting goods for others. Protects your liability in the event of damage to the property or goods.

- Bodily Injury Liability Insurance: This covers the costs associated with injuries or death caused to others in an accident where the truck driver is at fault.

- Trailer Interchange Insurance: This is required if you are transporting a trailer that does not belong to you. It covers damage to the trailer while it’s in your care, custody, and control.

- Non-Trucking Liability Insurance: If you are an owner-operator who is leasing your truck to a carrier, you’ll likely need non-trucking liability insurance (also known as bobtail insurance) to cover your vehicle when it is used for personal purposes or while empty.

Although Texas regulations may not always need certain coverages, the mentioned coverages are required. Depending on the nature of your trucking business, you need to include coverage like Workers’ Compensation, Cargo Insurance, and Physical Damage Insurance in your policy.

However, consulting with a trucking agency or brokers before choosing a policy is usually a good idea.

What are the things to consider when determining the most appropriate trucking insurance liability?

In Texas, trucking insurance providers will ask three key questions to help determine the most appropriate truck insurance and liability limits:

- What type of trucking operation do you run?

- What types of trucks and equipment do you use?

- What are your coverage and liability requirements?

How much is commercial truck insurance in Texas?

On average, Texas truck insurance can range from $3,000 to $12,000 annually for a single truck, but it can be higher for fleets or trucks hauling higher-risk loads.

What do I need to operate a commercial truck in Texas?

To operate a commercial truck in Texas you must meet several legal and regulatory requirements about Commercial Driver License (CDL), Motor Carrier Enforcement, Electronic Clearance, Electronic Tolls, Electronic Credentialing, CMV Registration (IRP), CMV Fuel Use Taxation (IFTA) and other requirements as per the law.
