## What Is OB/GYN Medical Malpractice Insurance?

OB/GYN Medical Malpractice Insurance is a type of professional liability insurance designed specifically for obstetricians and gynecologists. It provides financial protection against claims of medical negligence, covering legal defense costs, settlements, and judgments. This insurance is vital due to the unique risks associated with obstetric and gynecological care.

## Why OB/GYNs Require Specialized Malpractice Insurance

#### A High-Risk Specialty

OB/GYNs face some of the highest lawsuit rates in medicine—nearly 83% are sued at least once in their careers. Claims in this field also tend to be higher than in most other specialties, making tailored insurance essential.

#### Financial Protection Against High Stakes

Even defending a dismissed case can cost tens of thousands of dollars. Settlements often exceed $450,000, and severe birth injuries can top $500,000. Specialised OB/GYN coverage helps pay legal fees, settlements, and lost income.

#### Safeguarding Professional Reputation

Insurance also supports professional credibility, helping manage sensitive cases, hospital privileges, and public relations challenges.

#### Peace of Mind and Better Care

With the right coverage, OB/GYNs can focus on patient care, confident that their practice and finances are protected.

## OB/GYN Malpractice Insurance Costs

OB/GYNs often pay some of the highest malpractice premiums in medicine. In major cities, annual costs can range from $100,000 to $200,000. High premiums lead many to limit high-risk deliveries, reduce gynecological procedures, move from private practice to hospitals, or relocate to areas with lower liability risk.

## Factors Driving Malpractice Costs

Geographic Location: Premiums vary by state and city based on legal risks and claim rates. For example:

- New York: Some OB/GYNs pay upwards of $226,000 annually.

- Michigan: The average rate is approximately $89,533 annually.

- Virginia: Average rates range from $57,153 to $85,620 for $1M/$3M coverage.

Years in Practice: Early-career OB/GYNs may pay less, but premiums rise with more complex cases.

Claims History: Past claims can increase costs, while a clean record can lower them.

Subspecialty: High-risk areas, like maternal-fetal medicine, often carry higher premiums.

Coverage Limits: Standard policies are $1M per claim / $3M aggregate. Higher limits cost more but offer stronger protection.

## Medical Malpractice Insurance Challenges for OB/GYNs

OB/GYNs face unique challenges that make malpractice insurance essential.

#### Shortage of OB/GYN Providers

Nearly 50% of U.S. counties have no practising OB/GYN (ACOG). This limits patient access, increases wait times, and puts extra pressure on available doctors, raising the risk of errors.

#### Rising Malpractice Insurance Costs

OB/GYN malpractice premiums are among the highest of any specialty, sometimes up to a third of income. High-risk deliveries and birth complications drive costs, leading some doctors to focus only on gynecology.

#### High Stress and Burnout Risks

Long hours, on-call duties, and emotionally intense cases create chronic stress. Malpractice insurance, combined with risk management, offers a safety net to protect both practice and well-being.

## Choosing the Right OB/GYN Malpractice Policy

Selecting the appropriate malpractice insurance is crucial for OB/GYNs to safeguard their practice and personal assets. Here's how to make an informed choice:

###### Assess Your Practice Type

Are you in solo practice, part of a group, or hospital-employed? Each setup has different coverage needs.

###### Evaluate Carrier Reputation

Choose insurers with strong financial stability and a proven track record defending OB/GYNs.

###### Understand Policy Details

Know the deductibles, exclusions, and retroactive dates. Claims-made policies may not cover incidents before the start date without prior acts coverage.

###### Consult an Experienced Broker

Work with brokers experienced in OB/GYN malpractice to compare carriers, clarify terms, and ensure the best coverage for your practice.
