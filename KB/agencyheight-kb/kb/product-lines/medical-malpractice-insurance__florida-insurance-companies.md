Medical malpractice insurance is critical for healthcare professionals and patients alike. Whether you are a doctor looking for coverage or a client seeking peace of mind, knowing the best medical malpractice insurance companies can help you make an informed decision.

## Florida Malpractice Insurance Overview

Florida law mandates that doctors show financial responsibility to cover medical claims. They can achieve this by:

- Obtaining an irrevocable letter of credit, or

- Keeping funds in an escrow account.

However, unlike auto insurance, there is no strict enforcement system to ensure doctors carry malpractice insurance. This creates a gap in accountability.

Florida also stands out for having some of the highest premiums for malpractice insurance due to its generous jury verdicts. For example, premiums in areas like Miami-Dade County are notably higher than in other parts of the state.
