## What Is Garage Liability Insurance and Why You Need It

Garage liability insurance is designed specifically for businesses in the auto industry—repair shops, body shops, service centers, and dealerships. It protects you against claims of bodily injury or property damage that occur during your day-to-day operations.

Think of it as your safety net. While you’re focused on keeping vehicles running smoothly, this coverage makes sure one mistake, one accident, or one unhappy customer doesn’t derail your entire business.

Why It’s Essential:

- Auto repair shops are high-risk environments.

- Customers and employees move in and out all day.

- Heavy machinery, oil spills, and test drives can all lead to accidents.

Too many garage owners assume their general liability or property insurance has them covered. But without garage liability, there are dangerous gaps in protection that could cost you anything.

## Why Your Auto Business Cannot Operate Without It

Without garage liability coverage, even minor incidents can drain your finances or harm your reputation. This insurance ensures:

#### Customer Injuries Are Covered

Slip-and-fall accidents, burns, or minor injuries in your shop.

#### Property Damage Protection

Damage to vehicles or third-party property.

#### Legal Fees Are Included

Defense costs and settlements if sued.

#### Business Reputation Remains Intact

Demonstrates responsibility to clients.

## Garage Liability Insurance: What’s Covered - and What Isn’t

Your garage liability policy is your shield against the most common risks in your day-to-day business. This policy typically includes:

#### Bodily Injury

Covers medical expenses and legal fees if a customer is injured on your premises.

#### Property Damage

Pays for repairs or replacement if your business operations damage a customer’s property.

#### Legal Defense Costs

Covers attorney fees and court costs associated with covered claims.

#### Medical Payments

Pays for immediate medical expenses regardless of fault.

However, it's important to note that Garage Liability Insurance does not cover:

#### Damage to Customer Vehicles

For this, Garagekeepers Legal Liability Insurance is required.

#### Employee Injuries

These are covered under Workers’ Compensation Insurance.

#### Business Property Damage

Covered under Commercial Property Insurance

## What Affects the Cost of Garage Liability Insurance?

Insurance underwriters look at several factors when determining your premium. The most important include:

#### Business Revenue & Size

Higher revenue usually means more operations and greater exposure, which can increase your premium.

#### Type of Work Performed

High-risk services like collision repair, painting, or towing tend to cost more than routine maintenance.

#### Location / State

State regulations, accident rates, local crime levels, and healthcare costs all impact pricing.

#### Claims History

A record of past claims leads to higher rates, while a clean history helps keep costs down.

#### Coverage Limits & Deductibles

Choosing higher coverage limits (e.g., $1M per claim) or lower deductibles will raise your premium.

#### Business Size & Workforce

A larger staff usually means more vehicles serviced and more customer interactions, which increases your exposure to third-party claims.

## How to Choose the Right Garage Liability Policy for Your Business

Choosing the right garage liability insurance isn’t just about checking a box—it’s about understanding your risks, protecting your assets, and making informed decisions that safeguard your business for the long term. Here’s an educational guide to help you make the best choice:

#### Assess Your Business Risks

Every garage faces unique risks—from slips and falls on your premises to vehicle damage during service. Take time to identify all areas where your operations could create liability exposure. This step ensures your policy covers the scenarios that could financially impact your business.

#### Understand Coverage Limits and Their Importance

Coverage limits determine the maximum amount your insurer will pay per claim. A higher limit protects against costly lawsuits but may increase your premium. Consider:

- The value of vehicles in your care

- Potential medical or legal costs from customer injuries

- Industry standards and legal requirements

Understanding these factors helps you balance protection and cost-effectiveness.

#### Know What’s Included—and What’s Not

Not all garage liability policies are identical. Typical inclusions cover bodily injury, property damage, legal defense, and medical payments, but many policies exclude:

- Employee injuries (covered under workers’ comp)

- Damage to customer vehicles (requires garagekeepers coverage)

- Business property damage (covered under commercial property insurance)

Knowing these distinctions helps you fill coverage gaps with complementary policies, ensuring comprehensive protection.

Premiums, deductibles, and coverage details vary across insurers. Don’t just focus on price—compare:
