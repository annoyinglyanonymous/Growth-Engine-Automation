## How to Lower Car Insurance Costs?

There are a plethora of things you can do to decrease your car insurance premium. Here are some of the major actions you can take:

Increase your deductible

One of the simplest ways to reduce your premium is to raise your deductible if you can.

An insurance deductible is the amount you pay out of your pocket when filing a claim. When you are paying more on your own, your overall premium will decrease and vice versa. According to the Insurance Information Institute, you can save up to 30% on the cost of your collision and comprehensive coverage if you increase your deductible from $200 to $500.

Compare rates

Shop around and check the auto insurance premiums of multiple companies before finalizing on anyone. While doing so, don’t forget to also consider the local and regional insurers of your area.

Bundle your home and car insurance

When you get your home and car insurance from the same insurer or insure more than one vehicle from the same company, you might be eligible for discounts. You can save from 5% to 25% depending on the company. When buying from a single insurer, your entire insurance process is also more centralized and simpler

Inquire about all the discounts

There are a wide range of discounts provided by auto insurance companies. Find out which discounts apply to you.

You can get a discount if you have:

- Insured more than one vehicle with the same carrier

- Your car has the latest safety features and/or you have a clean driving record

- Your car is less than 3 years old

- You haven’t met an accident or traffic violation in a certain amount of time

- You have at least a B in your high school or college

Sign-up for usage-based car insurance

Many insurers have apps or devices that can track your driving behavior. By signing up for such programs, companies can accurately track your speed, braking habits, how much you drive and how well you drive.

Good drivers can benefit highly from these programs because instead of getting a premium set by the company, you can get a premium catered to your driving skills and record. And as a bonus, you will be encouraged to be safer overall.

Take a defensive driving course

If you take a defensive driving course approved by the company, you can get a few discounts. However, you need to make sure that your efforts and the cost involved for the course are greater than the savings you might secure. You also need to sign up for a course that has been accredited by the state.

Improve your credit score (if you can)

In many states, credit scores are often a base for insurance companies to determine premiums. When you boost your credit by reducing your debts, consistently making payments on time, preparing a monthly budget to track your bills, you can receive cheaper insurance rates.

Make your car safer

Many car insurance companies provide discounts on car insurance if you have safety systems like anti-theft devices installed in your car. A safer car equals lesser risks and hence reduces your premium.

Review your coverage thoroughly

Check your coverage properly, if possible, with an expert to evaluate which coverage you need and which ones you can do about. You can’t of course leave out the ones that might be useful to you, but some coverages like car rental coverage may not be applicable to

you. Pay attention to everything your car insurance policy offers so that you don’t include coverage you don’t need.

Be mindful of insurance costs when buying a new car

Every action counts when looking for ways to save insurance costs. Right from the moment you are browsing cars, you need to keep in mind the costs associated with the car you will be buying.

Factors like how safe the vehicle is, how likely it is for your car to be stolen, what are the repair costs of the car model and how often claims are filed for that specific car can be helpful in assessing how much it might cost to insure the car.
