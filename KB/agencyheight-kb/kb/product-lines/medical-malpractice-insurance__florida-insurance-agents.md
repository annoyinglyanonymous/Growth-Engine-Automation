## Florida Medical Malpractice Insurance Overview

Florida has gradually become a favorable market for medical malpractice insurance due to the tort reforms and the dropping premium rates despite the tumultuous landscape in the past.

Medical Malpractice Insurance is a type of professional liability insurance specifically designed for medical practitioners that protects medical workers financially against claims/lawsuits made by patients or their family for medical negligence.

Medical malpractice insurance provides financial protection for medical professionals against lawyer’ fees, court expenses and, if the professional ends up being guilty of negligence, payments for a settlement or judgment if the healthcare worker wants to settle out of court.

## Is medical malpractice insurance required in Florida?

Medical Malpractice Insurance is not compulsory by federal law, but it is highly recommended to get medical malpractice insurance in Florida to stay covered against damaging medical malpractice lawsuits that can be career-ruining.

## Who Needs Medical Malpractice Insurance?

Medical malpractice insurance is recommended to any healthcare professional working in the medical field.

This includes:

- Physicians and Surgeons Practicing in Texas

MDs, DOs, and specialists (e.g., cardiologists, surgeons, anesthesiologists).

- Healthcare Professionals

Nurse Practitioners (NPs), Physician Assistants (PAs), RNs, LVNs, CRNAs, and midwives.

- Hospitals, Clinics and Healthcare Facilities

Hospitals, surgical centers, rehab centers, and diagnostic labs.

- Mental Health Professionals

Psychiatrists, psychologists, counselors, and therapists.

- Allied Health Professionals

Chiropractors, optometrists, dentists, dental hygienists, and pharmacists.

- Telemedicine Providers

Professionals offering virtual care.

## Why Medical Malpractice Insurance is Crucial for Medical Professionals?

Getting Medical malpractice insurance over going bare for your practice is a no-brainer considering the consequences involved in case of a malpractice lawsuit and the financial woes the follow.

- A study by American Medical Association, revealed that more than 65 percent of doctors over the age of 55 claim to have been sued at least once in their career.

- The insurance coverage provided by a hospital is not sufficient to cover the individual professionals in case of a medical malpractice lawsuit.

- Medical malpractice insurance can protect your hard-earned personal assets as medical lawsuits can wipe them all away if you are not covered. Also, it covers your lost wages while you are fighting a lawsuit.

1. Does Florida have a medical malpractice cap?

No, there are no caps on economic damage in Florida. This implies there are no limitations on damages when it comes tosettling the compensation with the plaintiff for their medical payments and loss of incomedue to the medical malpractice.

2. What doctors pay the most for medical malpractice insurance?

Medical Professionals with riskiest specialties like neurosurgeons, cardiovascular surgeons, obstetricians, surgeons, and ER doctors pay more for medical malpractice insurance.

3. What are the types of medical malpractice insurance carriers?

The types of medical malpractice insurance carriers are admitted carriers, excess and surplus lines, risk retention groups, captives, trusts, and joint underwriting associations.
